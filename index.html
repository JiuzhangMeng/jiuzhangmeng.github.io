My research interests lie in systems neuroscience, particularly in understanding neural population activity and synaptic connectivity dynamics through multimodal approaches that combine electrophysiology and optical imaging. I completed my undergraduate studies at Capital Medical University （Beijing, CHina), where I investigated the relationship between synapse formation-related proteins and Alzheimer’s disease. I subsequently pursued a Master’s degree at the Kavli Institute for Systems Neuroscience (Trondheim, Norway), where I combined whole-cell patch-clamp recording with a modified MINI2P two-photon microscope to investigate theta-frequency neuronal firing in hippocampal pyramidal cells.
