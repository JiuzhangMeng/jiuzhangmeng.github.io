About

I completed my MSc in Neuroscience at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) in June 2026. My master's research combined whole-cell patch-clamp electrophysiology with two-photon calcium and voltage imaging to study CA1 pyramidal neurons in acute mouse hippocampal slices and evaluate a miniaturized two-photon imaging system.

My interests are in understanding how neural activity is measured, represented, and interpreted across neurons and circuits. I am particularly interested in systems and circuit neuroscience, neural population dynamics, in vivo electrophysiology, neural decoding, and technologies that connect neural signals with brain-computer interfaces.

Research experience

Combining electrophysiology and two-photon imaging in hippocampal CA1 neurons

MSc Research Project · Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU)

My master's project investigated a miniaturized two-photon imaging system by combining cellular imaging with simultaneous whole-cell patch-clamp recordings in acute mouse hippocampal slices. The work focused on calcium and voltage imaging in CA1 pyramidal neurons.

Methods: Whole-cell patch clamp, two-photon calcium and voltage imaging, ScanImage, Fiji/ImageJ, Clampfit, and data analysis in R.
